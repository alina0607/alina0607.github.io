About

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer at Autar, where I build deep learning and static-analysis systems that reason about code. My work spans a code-quality model trained on how software evolves across releases, an autonomous LLM-agent loop that adversarially verifies its own outputs and generates supervised training labels, and a cross-language analysis engine covering five languages.

I came to ML from systems engineering.
